DEOSAI NATIONAL PARK

Deosai National Park, also known as the "Land of Giants," is a high-altitude alpine plain located between the Skardu District and Astore District in Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan. Situated at an average elevation of 4,114 meters (13,497 feet) above sea level, it is the second highest plateau in the world after the Changtang Tibetan Plateau. The park is renowned for its stunning landscapes, rich flora and fauna, and the beautiful Sheosar Lake. It is home to a variety of wildlife, including the critically endangered Himalayan brown bear, which has seen a significant increase in population since the park's establishment in 19931. The park covers an area of 843 square kilometers (325 square miles) and is a haven for nature enthusiasts and adventure seekers. Deosai National Park is accessible from several routes, including Astore Valley, Skardu, and Galtari Tehsil. The best time to visit is during the summer months when the park is accessible and the wildflowers are in full bloom1
